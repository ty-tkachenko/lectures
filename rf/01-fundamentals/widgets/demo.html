Try this: (1) Pick Open end, then change εeff. The cut stays at the same distance, but the time it takes the echo to come back (top axis) changes. A real TDR only measures time, so if you guess εeff wrong you will look for the fault in the wrong place. (2) Pick the capacitive bump and slow the rise time to 200 ps. The dip gets shallower: a slow TDR can't see small things. (3) Pick Impedance section at 60 Ω and make it longer. In the lower plot the S11 ripples get closer together: ripple period = vp/(2 × length).

Polarization loss

Try this: with two linear antennas, set the tilts 90° apart. In theory nothing gets through. Now make one of them circular: the loss is 3 dB at every tilt. That's why a spinning rocket often carries a circular antenna, or why the ground station uses one.
