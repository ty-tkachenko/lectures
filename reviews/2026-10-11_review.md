### EE Module 4 scope

Agreed with RF & Antenna. Opening question: "why does a 10 µF capacitor stop being a capacitor at 50 MHz?"

- Phasors: a sinusoid as a rotating arrow, phase as a delay in fractions of a cycle.
- \(X_L = \omega L\) and \(X_C = -1/(\omega C)\) derived by applying \(v = L\,di/dt\) and \(i = C\,dv/dt\) to a sine.
- \(Z = R + jX\) read as "how much current, and how late".
- Complex ratios as magnitude plus angle, applied to a divider (the shape \(\Gamma\) takes later).
- dB and Bode plots, with the RC filter built from the impedance divider.
- Spectrum conceptually: a fast edge contains many frequencies, no Fourier math.
- RMS.
- Bare resonance only: L and C cancel at one frequency, with a capacitor's self-resonance as the example.

Q, bandwidth and matching are left to RF 2.

- [ ] **Module 4 written** to this scope, at the depth of Modules 1–3.
