Notes on the electronics behind our rockets, from Ohm's law to the telemetry link. Each module is a single page of explanation with interactive figures and worked examples from our own hardware, a lecture video that walks through it, a lab video showing the ideas at the bench, and design exercises.

The two series are meant to be read in order. The RF notes assume EE modules 1–4 and 12.

Hardware Engineering Fundamentals. Written for the avionics team.
