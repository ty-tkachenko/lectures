## Lab — step, filter, kickback

**Total: 14:00.**

5 V current limit. Coil current under 100 mA. Scope probe on ×10. Diode fitted before the undioded shot, and the scope range already high enough to see a spike.

| Time | Duration | Shot / talking point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | 1:30 | Three checks: time to 63% on an RC step, −3 dB on a low-pass, then a coil with a diode and one look without it. Say the current limit. |
| 1:30 | 3:30 | 10 kΩ and 100 nF. Slow square wave, 0 to 5 V. Probe the capacitor, ground at the capacitor. Predict τ = 1.00 ms and 63% at about 3.2 V. Put the cursors where the curve actually crosses, and read them. Mention capacitor tolerance if you are off. |
| 5:00 | 3:30 | Swap to 1.6 kΩ and 100 nF, output on the capacitor. Sine in. Hold the input amplitude fixed. Find 0.707 times that amplitude. Read the frequency. Ideal fc is 995 Hz. If the generator is 50 Ω and it is in series with the resistor, say that the cutoff will shift. |
| 8:30 | 2:30 | Small coil, 5 V, series resistor, current under 100 mA. Diode across the coil, cathode to the supply side. Scope the switch node. Expect about one diode drop above the supply. Say 0.7 V is a round assumption if you use it. |
| 11:00 | 2:00 | Confirm the scope range. Lift the diode. One shot. Read the peak the screen shows. Do not quote 200 V unless that is the number on the screen. Put the diode back. |
| 13:00 | 1:00 | Takeaways. The blank table on the reading page is the student's copy of this. |
