Drag the red dot along input voltage, or use the sliders. The pass element drops Vin − Vout at the load current, and that product is heat.

Try this: (1) The defaults start in continuous conduction, near the 8.4 V to 3.3 V example. Read ΔIL, ΔVout, and the duty. (2) Shrink the inductor or the switching frequency until the valley hits zero. (3) That is discontinuous conduction. The continuous-conduction ripple formula stops drawing the real shape.
