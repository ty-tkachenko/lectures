# EE

General electrical engineering for rocket-club members: circuits, parts, power, and boards. A newcomer can start at any module that is already written. A club project may show up as an example or a practical. It is not the subject of the series.

The boundary with the RF series is a 50 Ω trace. Controlled impedance, matching, antennas, link budgets, noise-figure cascades, and radio register maps live under [RF]({{ '/rf/' | relative_url }}).

## Outline

Twelve modules. A title is a link only when that module's reading page exists. The others are not written yet.

1. **01 Circuit Fundamentals.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: why does a circuit draw current, and what does a battery voltage actually promise?
2. **02 Capacitors and Inductors in Circuits.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: why does a rail dip when a radio keys up, and what stores the energy that fills the dip?
3. **03 Diodes and Transistors.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: how do you block a backwards battery, or switch a load, without a mechanical relay?
4. **04 Op Amps and Analog Building Blocks.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: how do you turn a tiny sensor voltage into a signal a flight computer can trust?
5. **[05 Power Conversion: Linear vs Switching]({{ '/ee/05-power-conversion/' | relative_url }}).** Opens with: why does a 3.3 V flight computer get hot when it runs from a higher pack voltage, and when should the regulator switch instead of throwing the spare volts away as heat?
6. **06 Batteries and Power Systems.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: how long will the pack last, and what changes as the cells sag from full to empty?
7. **07 Signals, Sampling and ADCs.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: when you sample a sensor, what did the conversion throw away?
8. **08 Digital Logic and Interfaces.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: how do chips on one board agree on a bit, and what fails when the wires get longer?
9. **09 Real Components.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Why parts are not ideal, how to read a datasheet, and how to source a part you can actually buy. Opens with: why doesn't the part on the board match the ideal symbol?
10. **10 From Schematic to Working Board.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> Opens with: what has to happen between a finished schematic and a board that powers on?
11. **11 Reading a Real Schematic.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> A guided walkthrough. Opens with: can you read a real schematic and say what each block is doing?
12. **12 Reading a Real Layout.** <span class="outline-soon">Coming soon.</span> A guided walkthrough. Opens with: can you look at a real layout and see where the current actually flows?
